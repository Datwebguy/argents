ON-CHAIN SAFETY

Security & guardrails

VERIFIED ON ARC

Most AI agent applications are simple prompt loops tied to raw private keys. Argents enforces security directly in smart contracts, not just text prompts.

You set the rules and maintain ownership; your agent operates safely within your specified boundaries.

01

Humans as Owners, Agents as Operators

Your agent never holds master keys. It operates within an isolated smart vault where withdrawal limits and key controls require your authenticated owner approval.

02

24-Hour Spending Limits

No infinite allowances. Daily outflows are strictly capped by on-chain math that resets at midnight UTC, ensuring your maximum downside is strictly bounded.

03

Exact Minor-Unit Accounting

All token math is computed in integer base units (6 decimal places for native Arc USDC). Zero floating-point rounding errors.

04

Separation of Idle vs. Deployed Capital

Idle cash is kept distinct from Morpho yield shares on-chain, ensuring liquidity recall is deterministic and transparent.

05

On-Chain Events as Single Source of Truth

No private off-chain databases to crash or get out of sync. Every movement of funds is an immutable log on the Arc blockchain.

06

Direct Trust-Minimized Settlement

In Signals mode, payments and delivery receipts are permanently recorded on Arc without middlemen.

07

Zero Centralized API Keys

Argents has no server-side secrets or centralized database credentials. All reads use public Arc RPC nodes, and all actions are signed directly by your wallet.

⚖
The Golden Rule of Autonomous Capital:

Never ask: “Is my autonomous agent safe?”
Always ask: “What is the maximum mathematical loss this agent can cause in 24 hours if something goes wrong?”

With Argents, that limit is a hard number enforced by a verified smart contract on Arc.
